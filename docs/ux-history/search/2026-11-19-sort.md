# Search — results by date, sort and how close a match (2026-11-19, iOS)

Tag: `ux/search/2026-11-19`

Asked: search results as a feed by date with a place filter, like Moments; then a sort with Most relevant (every
match) and Newest / Oldest first (only matches over a threshold the user sets). Screenshots (dark) of the second step
are in [2026-11-19-sort/](2026-11-19-sort/).

## Options
- **a — Feed by date, place chips**: the matches grouped by day like Moments, a Newest first | Best match switch and a
  row of town chips with counts. *Kept as the base* of a2–a4.
- **b — Events**: each day in one town a card (town, date, count) that opens in place; a Place pill and sheet.
  *Turned down* for a.
- **c — Map and feed**: a map of the matches' towns on top; tapping a bubble narrows the feed under it. *Turned down*
  for a.
- **a2 — Sort menu, Match slider**: a Sort menu (Most relevant · Newest first · Oldest first) and, for the date
  orders, a "How close a match" slider on the page. *Turned down* for a3.
- **a3 — Sort sheet with a preview of the cut**: a Sort pill above the field ("↓ Newest first · 29"), as the Filter
  pill, opens a sheet: the three orders as rows with a line each, and for the date orders the slider, the count, and
  the weakest photos the cut still keeps, then Show N photos. The page is the title, the town chips and the results.
  *Picked* (Nov 19, 2026).
- **a4 — Most relevant | By date ↓↑, three steps**: a two-way switch, tapping By date again turns the order; Close ·
  Good · Loose chips with counts instead of a slider. *Turned down* for a3.

## What they faked
- All: the answer to "concerts" was every third photo of the lab's places library (`SearchLabConcerts`); the town
  chips filtered what was shown instead of asking the server; photos didn't open.
- a2–a4: the scores were made up per photo (`SearchLabSorting`); the app's search keeps no scores and sorts best
  first only. Thresholds and the slider's range are guesses to tune on real answers.
- c: the map needed the network for its tiles (MapKit).

    git checkout ux/search/2026-11-19 -- iosApp/iosApp/Lab/Search/Option<B>

lab). Built against `LibraryColors`, `FeedPhoto` and the lab's places library as of the tag.
