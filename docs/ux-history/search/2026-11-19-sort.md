# Search — results by date, sorting and a match threshold (2026-11-19, iOS)

Tag: `ux/search/2026-11-19` (at commit f3b3397, every option still in it). Screenshots of the second step:
[2026-11-19-sort/](2026-11-19-sort/).

hen asked to search for something like "concerts" and see the results as a feed sorted by date, filtered by place.
Search answers best first, with a year and town filter. The first step (options a, b, c) laid out a feed by date.
hen then specified: sort by date, new to old or old to new, with a search threshold to tweak; or most relevant, and
then no threshold.

## Options
- **a — Feed by date, place chips**: Newest first | Best match switch, a row of town chips, the matches as Moments
  days. *Superseded* by the second step.
- **b — Event cards**: a day in one town as a card that opens in place, a Place pill and sheet. *Turned down*.
- **c — Map and feed**: a small map of the matches' towns on top, a tap narrows the feed by date. *Turned down*.
- **a2 — Sort menu, Match slider**: a Sort menu (Most relevant · Newest first · Oldest first) under the title and a
  "How close a match" slider under it in the date orders. *Turned down*.
- **a3 — Sort sheet with a preview of the cut**: a Sort pill above the field ("↓ Newest first · 29") opens a sheet with
  the three orders (each with a line on what it does), the slider in the date orders, "29 photos. The weakest kept:"
  with the four weakest photos the cut keeps, and Show 29 photos. *Picked* (hen, Nov 19, 2026: "the sheet with
  slider"). Promoted in c13accd4 (Android and iOS, with the town chips kept).
- **a4 — Most relevant | By date ↓↑, three steps**: a second tap on By date turns the order; Close · Good · Loose with
  their counts instead of a slider. *Turned down*.

## What they faked
- All: the answer to "concerts" (every third photo of the places library) and the scores (made up per photo, 0.04 to
  0.26). The app doesn't ask the server for `SearchHit.score` yet, and sorts best first only.
- a, a2–a4: the town chips filter what is shown in place; the app's town filter asks the server again.
- a3: the slider's range (0.04 to 0.26) and starting point (0.14) are guesses to tune on real answers.
