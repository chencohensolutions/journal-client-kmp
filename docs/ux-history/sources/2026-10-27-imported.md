# Show in the app — imported files apart from the phones' folders (2026-10-27, Android)

Tag: `ux/sources/2026-10-27`

A WhatsApp photo showed as Other on the phone while its sidecar on the server said `whatsapp`: the phone tells a file's
source by its folder only, and the import tells it by the folder and WhatsApp's file names (`IMG-…-WA….jpg`). The owner
asked not to merge the phone's WhatsApp folder with the import's WhatsApp, and to have an "Imported" section with the
import's kinds instead. Each option had a Settings › Show in the app page and a `-bar` page (the Moments filter bar,
with the "Showing" sheet drawn open under it).

## Options
- **a — Two groups**: "From your phones" and "Imported" as two sets of pills, each with a line on what it holds;
  imported pills carry a box icon; in the bar, imported chips after a divider and "Imported". *Turned down* for b.
- **b — Imported as one source**: one "Imported · 54,749" pill beside the phone's folders, hiding all imported files at
  once; while on, a tinted box under the pills, "Of what was imported", holds its kinds (Camera, WhatsApp,
  Screenshots, Other); one "Imported" chip in the bar. *Liked*, then changed into b2 to show Private settings.
- **b2 — Imported as one source, with Private settings**: b, with what Private settings (Hidden collections) hides on
  every device said in place rather than switched here: a kind it hides whole (WhatsApp) is a dimmed pill with a lock
  that opens Private settings, one it hides in part (screenshots of chosen apps) has a lock after its count, and every
  count is only what reaches the phone; a line under the pills, "🔒 Hidden on all your devices: WhatsApp, 1,640 more
  Screenshots. Not counted here." with **Private settings ›**. The bar has no chip and no "+N" for what Private settings
  hides whole. The idea: Show in the app chooses by *where a file came from*, on this phone; Private settings hides by
  *what a file is*, on every device, and stays the one place that really hides. *Picked*; not promoted yet.
- **c — Table**: a row per kind, a "Phones" and an "Imported" column, each cell switched on its own with its count, a
  dash where a kind has none; bar chips marked with a phone or a box. *Turned down* for b.

## What they faked
- All: the app doesn't know which files were imported. The server says it (`Media.uploadedBy` is `"import"`), but the
  library doesn't keep it; the counts were made up, the switches on the page didn't change, and toggles were kept only
  while an option was open.
- b2: how many files of a source Private settings hides, split by phones and imported. The server leaves them out of
  the feed, and `hiddenCollections` counts all of a user's files without that split. "Private settings ›" and the
  locked pills did nothing.
- c: an imported folder ("Samsung Notes"): the import keeps only the four kinds today.
