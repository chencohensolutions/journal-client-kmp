# Settings — Models on this phone (2026-11-08, Android)

Tag: `ux/settings/2026-11-08`

The Android app stopped carrying MediaPipe's photo embedder (10.9 MB) and downloads it from the user's server on first
use (`phoneModels`). The owner asked for a way in Settings to download the models the app needs. Screenshots of every
option are in [docs/screens/mockups/models/](../../screens/mockups/models/) (`a*.png`, `b*.png`, `c*.png`).

## Options
- **a — A page of its own**: "Models on this phone", from a row under Search on Settings' first page ("1 of 2
  downloaded"): every model with what it does, how it stands (Downloaded, Not downloaded, Downloading 112 MB of 290 MB
  with a bar, Waiting for Wi-Fi, Couldn't download, Your server doesn't offer it yet) and its button (Download,
  Download now, Pause, Try again, Delete); Download all; Only on Wi-Fi; the space they take; a line when the server
  can't be reached. *Picked*, promoted as `ModelsScreen.kt` (`real-a.png`, `real-a-downloading.png`, `real-a-row.png`).
- **b — A card in Search**: the same list as a Models card in Settings › Search, above On device search, whose Only
  on Wi-Fi it would use. *Turned down* for a.
- **c — Beside its feature**: the photo model as a card in Settings › Moments under Sets of similar photos, saying
  what sets do without it; search's model left in On device search. *Turned down* for a.

## What they faked
- All: made-up states (`LabModelsState` in the lab), no downloads; the sizes were the real ones (10.9 MB, 290 MB
  8-bit text model, 440 MB index). B drew the real On device search section below its card; C drew a copy of the
  How alike card.

## Reviving an option
    git checkout ux/settings/2026-11-08 -- androidApp/src/debug/kotlin/com/chencohensolution/journal/ui/library/lab/models androidApp/src/debug/res/values/strings_models.xml androidApp/src/debug/res/values-iw/strings_models.xml
Then add its options to `LabScreens` (as in the tag). Its strings share names with the app's `models_*` strings now:
rename the lab's before reviving.
