# Backup — copies on other disks (2026-10-28, Android)

Tag: `ux/backup/2026-10-28-2`

The server can keep plain copies of each user's data folder on other disks (journal-server-go/docs/copies.md), but
only the admin commands show how they are doing. The owner, about to turn copies on, asked for a place in Settings ›
Backup that shows how many files reached each copy.

## Options
- **a — A row, then a page**: a "Copies on other disks" row at the end of the top card, under On your server, with
  how they all are in a line ("1 of 2 copies up to date", "Copying to E:\…", "Needs a look: F:\hen" in red with a
  dot). It opens a page with a card per copy: its folder, the files it holds in large type ("49,377 of 49,414
  files"), a bar, its state, warnings (files that failed, a copy on the server's own disk), then when it runs, its
  last pass, the disk's free space, files only in the copy, and Copy now. *Picked*, promoted in b1e7943.
- **b — Cards on Backup**: a "Copies on other disks" section under the top card, also with backup off, a card per
  copy with the files, a bar and the state in a line; a tap shows the rest and Copy now. *Turned down* for a.
- **c — Where your files are**: in place of On your server, one line down the top card from This phone to Your
  server to each copy, a dot per place (filled when it has everything, a ring while files are on their way, red when
  it can't be reached), "Kept in 3 places" above it; a tap on a copy shows the rest. *Turned down* for a.

## What they faked
- All: the copies (the app can't ask: `copyReport` and `startCopy` are in the admin schema only); Copy now did
  nothing; the times were made up from now.
- a: the page wasn't asked again while open.
